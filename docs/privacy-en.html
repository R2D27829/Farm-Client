Privacy policy

Version: 2026-10-16 · Website, launcher, mod and Android app

Your account, settings and communications: what is processed and how to contact us.

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes data used by the website, Farm ID, launcher, mod and Farm Client Android app. Contact the project administration through support for access, correction or deletion requests. Never post passwords, payment credentials or session tokens in public requests.

2. Data used

  • Farm ID, nickname, email when registering, linked Discord, Telegram or VK account information and privacy preferences.
  • License, activation, device/HWID and game-session information to verify access and prevent abuse. Website registration alone does not require an HWID.
  • Messages and attachments, posts, comments, clans, cloud configurations and support requests that you submit; presence and game activity within enabled features.
  • Technical request information, active-session information and diagnostics you send to support. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and request logs.

3. Purposes

Account registration and sign-in, paid access, configuration sync, communication and community features, support, security and abuse prevention. Purchases may create order and payment-status records. Do not send full payment-card details to the project in messages.

4. Passwords, sessions and local storage

The server uses a salted password hash, not plaintext password storage. The website stores a sign-in token and settings in your browser; the launcher and app may store tokens and settings on your device. Clearing browser data signs you out locally but does not delete your server account. Do not share session files. The cookie notice concerns necessary local storage and does not itself enable advertising cookies.

5. Visibility

Public profiles, posts, comments and public community information may be visible to others. Social-link and presence privacy can be changed in your profile. Messages are available to conversation participants; the project does not claim end-to-end encryption. Administrators process data for service administration and support. Do not publish someone else’s personal information without permission.

6. Third-party services

The website and downloads use GitHub; the API uses Cloudflare. Depending on features you choose, the product contacts sign-in providers, Minecraft/Microsoft, Ely.by, Modrinth, CurseForge and music services. Those providers’ policies apply to their own processing. External fonts and media may load from third-party servers. Processing may take place outside your country.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

Data is kept to the extent and for the period needed for features, security, support and applicable legal requirements. A single retention period for every category has not been established. You can edit profile and privacy settings, revoke sessions in Devices, delete content where controls are available, or request access, correction and deletion through support. Account ownership may need to be verified. Certain records may be retained on a lawful basis; backup-deletion timing is clarified when responding to a request.

8. Security and updates

Access checks and technical safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password and sign out on shared devices. Revisions are published here. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.
